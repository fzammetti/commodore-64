About this reconstruction

Fantasy and Infiltrator’s July 1990 cooperative demo, reconstructed from the supplied aura_coop.prg. The artwork, character sets, scroll messages, animation tables, and music register sequences come from that program. Both pages run as native JavaScript inside Phaser 3.88.2, not as a C64 emulator or a video.

Space follows the original sequence: welcome → Fantasy → Infiltrator. The numbered page selectors, pause, restart, and fullscreen controls are additions. The welcome screen’s telephone number is historical. Audio uses a browser SID-style synthesizer; analog filtering and cycle-level raster details are approximations. Timing targets NTSC: 263 raster lines, 65 CPU cycles per line, approximately 59.83 frames per second.

Fantasy’s music loop was verified against the recovered player state. Infiltrator’s music replays 50,000 captured frame updates before restarting; its complete player-state loop was not established.
