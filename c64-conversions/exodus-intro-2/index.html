About this reconstruction

"Intro #2" from the Exodus disk, which Fantasy wrote for the group in one day on 11/24/88, reconstructed from the supplied Commodore 64 program. The original logos, character sets, texts, scroll text and music are preserved. Phaser presents JavaScript translations of the recovered raster interrupts.

As on the C64, SPACE leaves the intro. It was written to run a game that is not on this disk, so it drops to an empty BASIC READY prompt, just as it does in an emulator. SPACE or R then runs the intro again.

Sound starts after your first interaction. Animation follows NTSC timing and 263 raster lines, with the colour splits placed at the cycles traced in VICE. The SID sound is an approximation. The browser controls are additions.
