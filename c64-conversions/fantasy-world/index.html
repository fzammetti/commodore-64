About this reconstruction

Fantasy World, recreated for the browser from the supplied Commodore 64 program, fantasy_world.prg. Built with Phaser, with a presentation shell adapted from the neighboring Aura reconstruction. The supplied PRG is preserved; four damaged bytes were corrected in a separate analysis copy using the archived release.

The surrounding controls are browser additions. Use Space to advance between sections. Sound starts after a click or key press. Music captures restart after about three minutes.
