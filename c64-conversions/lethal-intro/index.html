About this reconstruction

The "make-shift intro" Fantasy wrote for Lethal's crack of New Barbarian II, reconstructed from the supplied Commodore 64 program. The original character set, texts, scroll text and music are preserved. Phaser presents JavaScript translations of the recovered raster interrupt.

As on the C64, SPACE leaves the intro: it resets the screen and copies the game into place. The game itself is not part of this reconstruction, so it stops on that blue screen, where New Barbarian II would start. SPACE or R then runs the intro again.

Sound starts after your first interaction. Animation follows NTSC timing and 263 raster lines, with the raster bars and the starfield's stores placed at the cycles traced in VICE. The SID sound is an approximation. The browser controls are additions.
