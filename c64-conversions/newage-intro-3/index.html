About this reconstruction

The intro Fantasy wrote for NewAge's release of Psynus V1.1 (April 27, 1988), reconstructed from the supplied Commodore 64 program. The original character set, logo, texts, both scroll texts and the music are preserved. Phaser presents JavaScript translations of the recovered main loop, raster interrupts and music player.

As on the C64, any key leaves the intro: it resets the C64, clears the screen and copies the game into place. The game itself is not part of this reconstruction, so it stops on the black screen where Psynus would start. Like the original, the exit sometimes hangs, leaving the texts frozen without their colours. A key or R then runs the intro again.

Sound starts after your first interaction. Animation follows NTSC timing and 263 raster lines, with every raster colour and store placed at the cycle VICE runs it. The music, which the original plays from its main loop rather than from an interrupt, keeps that loop's uneven tempo; after about 2¾ minutes the tune ends, leaving a faint hiss of its last note, as on the C64. One cleanup: the logo's X scroll changes only between lines, which removes the stray pixels the original shows at the edges of its blocks. The SID sound is an approximation. The browser controls are additions.
