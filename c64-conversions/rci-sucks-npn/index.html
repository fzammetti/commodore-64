About this reconstruction

RCI Sucks by NPN, reconstructed from the supplied Commodore 64 program. The original graphics, fonts, sprite patterns, and scroll messages are preserved. Phaser presents JavaScript translations of the recovered routines.

The opening curtain responds to SPACE after it finishes opening. The Next part button skips directly ahead. Part 2 offers five bounce patterns. F1 returns to the opening.

Sound starts after your first interaction. Animation follows NTSC timing and 263 raster lines. Raster effects and analog SID sound are approximations. The browser controls are additions.

The portrait section, including its music, is restored from the replacement PRG. The opening and final letter are silent in the original. Analysis and fidelity notes.
