About this reconstruction

The SFI intro by Excelsior of ShadowForce International (1989), reconstructed from the supplied Commodore 64 program. The original logo, character sets, sprite animations, scroll texts, messages and music are preserved. Phaser presents JavaScript translations of the recovered raster interrupts and message routine.

As on the C64, SPACE leaves the intro. It was written to run a game that is not on this disk, so it drops to BASIC with a syntax error, just as it does in an emulator. SPACE or R then runs the intro again.

Sound starts after your first interaction. Animation follows NTSC timing and 263 raster lines, with the colour bars placed at the cycles traced in VICE. The SID sound is an approximation. The browser controls are additions. Analysis and fidelity notes.
