About this reconstruction

Stetsa, by Fantasy of Newage, reconstructed from the supplied Commodore 64 disk image. Original graphics, fonts, scroll messages, and vector coordinates are preserved. Phaser presents native JavaScript translations of the recovered routines.

The surrounding controls are browser additions. Sound starts after your first interaction. The interlude advances automatically. The final wireframe animation loops.

Animation uses the NTSC clock and 263 raster lines. Raster timing and analog SID sound are approximations. Credits and the first part restart their music after five minutes; the other three songs use verified loops. See README.md and analysis/ANALYSIS.md for details.
