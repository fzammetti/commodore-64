# TEA Intromaker I

A Phaser reconstruction of TEA Intromaker I, a Commodore 64 intro by Excelsior (my later handle after Fantasy) of SFI
for The Eastern Alliance (October 1989).  It includes the intro and the attached Intro/Editor I. The intro has three
raster-bar scrollers, color-cycling titles and the recovered SID tune. In the editor, you type the scroll texts and
save the result.  The Compactor V4/S title runs up to the linker's LOW-MEM prompt. The browser runs JavaScript
translations of the original routines, without a C64 emulator.

Timing uses **NTSC: 1,022,730 Hz, 65 cycles × 263 lines, about 59.826 frames/s**. The music runs at the NTSC
frame rate, so it plays faster than on PAL.
