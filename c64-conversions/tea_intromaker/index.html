About this reconstruction

TEA Intromaker I by Excelsior of SFI for The Eastern Alliance, reconstructed from the supplied Commodore 64 program. The intro's font, screen, scroll texts, color tables, and music are recovered from the program. Phaser presents JavaScript translations of its routines.

As on the C64, SPACE leaves the intro for the attached Intro/Editor I. In the editor, the keyboard types into the C64: choose 1–6, type a scroll text ending in @, and press RETURN. Saving downloads the INTRO file the original would write to disk, $0800–$2DFF with a $0800 load address.

Option 6 runs Compactor V4/S as far as its LOW-MEM prompt. The linking after that prompt needs a disk drive, so this reconstruction does not include it. The button that previews the edited intro is a browser addition. Animation follows NTSC timing and 263 raster lines. Analog SID sound is an approximation. Analysis and fidelity notes.
