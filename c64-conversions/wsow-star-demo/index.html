About this reconstruction

Star Demo by WSOW (We Share Our Warez), coded by Ice, reconstructed from the supplied Commodore 64 disk. The Koala picture is revealed one pixel row at a time. The scroller then runs as eight multicolor sprites in the opened lower border. The original picture, sprite font and message are preserved, including the garbage sprites for the message's digits.

The music is from The Last Ninja. As in the original, it begins with the scroller and follows the CIA timer: 52.85 calls per second on an NTSC machine. It has been recorded from the program's player and plays as SID register writes, looping where the tune loops.

Sound starts after your first interaction. Animation follows NTSC timing and 263 raster lines. The original takes no input. Skipping the reveal and the browser controls are additions. Analog SID sound is an approximation.
